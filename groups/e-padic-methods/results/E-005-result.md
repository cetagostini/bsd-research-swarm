# E-005 · p-adic Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer Conjecture (vis-à-vis Classical)

## Direction ID and Title
E-005: The p-adic Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture — its precise formulation, comparison with classical BSD, and the role of the p-adic period $\Omega_p(E)$.

## Status Assessment
**Conjectural in general; established for rank $\leq 1$ under standard hypotheses.** Mazur–Tate–Teitelbaum (1986) formulated p-adic BSD. Kato (2004) established the $\leq$ direction (Selmer group containment). Skinner–Urban (2014) proved the Iwasawa main conjecture for $\text{GL}_2$ under technical conditions, yielding p-BSD when $\text{ord}_{s=1} L(E,s) \leq 1$.

## Testable Conjecture or Lemma

**Conjecture (p-adic BSD).** Let $E/\mathbb{Q}$ be an elliptic curve with good ordinary reduction at an odd prime $p$, $r = \text{rank}(E(\mathbb{Q}))$. Then:

$$\frac{L_p^{(r)}(E, 1)}{r!} = \frac{|\Sha(E/\mathbb{Q})[p^\infty]|}{|E(\mathbb{Q})_{\text{tors}}|^2} \cdot \text{Reg}_p(E) \cdot \prod_{\ell | N} c_\ell^{(p)} \cdot \mathcal{E}_p(E)$$

where:
- $L_p(E, s)$ is the cyclotomic p-adic L-function
- $\text{Reg}_p(E) = \det(\hat{h}_p(P_i, P_j))$ is the p-adic regulator
- $c_\ell^{(p)}$ are p-adic Tamagawa numbers (the p-part of $c_\ell$)
- $\mathcal{E}_p(E)$ is the exceptional zero correction factor (equals 1 when no exceptional zero)

**Comparison Theorem (formal equivalence).** Under the compatibility $L_p(E, 1) = \mathcal{E}_p(E) \cdot L(E,1)/\Omega_E$ and the p-adic period relation:
$$\frac{\text{Reg}_p(E)}{\text{Reg}_\infty(E)} = \frac{\Omega_p(E)}{\Omega_E^+} \cdot (\text{p-adic unit})$$
the p-adic BSD formula and classical BSD are equivalent modulo p-power factors, provided:
1. The p-adic period $\Omega_p(E) \in \mathbb{Q}_p^\times$ is correctly normalised.
2. The Tamagawa numbers satisfy $c_\ell^{(p)} = v_p(c_\ell)$ (the p-adic valuation of the archimedean Tamagawa number).
3. The $\Sha$ factor satisfies $|\Sha[p^\infty]| = v_p(|\Sha|)$ (the p-part of the Tate–Shafarevich group).

## Approach Summary

1. **Iwasawa main conjecture:** The characteristic ideal of the Pontryagin dual $\text{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q}_\infty)^*$ over $\Lambda = \mathbb{Z}_p[[\text{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}_\infty/\mathbb{Q})]]$ is generated by $L_p(E, s)$ (viewed as an element of $\Lambda$ via the Mellin transform).

2. **Kato's Euler system:** The Beilinson–Kato elements $\mathbf{z}_{\text{BK}} \in H^1(\mathbb{Q}, T_p(E) \otimes \mathbb{Q}_p/\mathbb{Z}_p)$ provide the inequality $\text{char}(\text{Sel}^*) \supseteq (L_p)$, i.e., $|\Sha[p^\infty]|$ is controlled by $L_p(E, 1)$.

3. **Skinner–Urban:** Under hypotheses ($p$ split in an imaginary quadratic CM field, analytic rank $\leq 1$), the reverse inequality holds, giving the full main conjecture and hence p-BSD.

4. **p-adic Hodge theory:** The comparison $\text{Reg}_p / \text{Reg}_\infty$ involves the p-adic period $\Omega_p(E)$ defined via Fontaine's $B_{\text{dR}}$:
$$\Omega_p(E) = \frac{\text{det}(H^1_{\text{dR}}(E/\mathbb{Q}) / F^1, \text{comp.})}{\text{period}}$$
via the comparison isomorphism $H^1_{\text{dR}}(E/\mathbb{Q}) \otimes B_{\text{dR}} \cong H^1_{\text{ét}}(E_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}}, \mathbb{Q}_p) \otimes B_{\text{dR}}$.

## Computational Example

**Curve: $E = 37a1$** ($y^2 + y = x^3 - x$, conductor 37, rank 1).

**Classical BSD:**
- $L'(E, 1) \approx 0.30599$
- $\Omega_E^+ \approx 2.99373$ (real period)
- $\text{Reg}_\infty(E) = \hat{h}(P) \approx 0.05111$ (archimedean height of generator $P = (0,0)$)
- $c_\ell$: only bad prime is 37, $c_{37} = 1$ (Kodaira type $I_1$)
- $\Sha$: conjecturally trivial, $|\Sha| = 1$
- $|E(\mathbb{Q})_{\text{tors}}| = 1$
- Classical BSD: $L'(E,1)/1! = \frac{1 \cdot 0.05111 \cdot 1 \cdot 2.99373}{1} \approx 0.15305$... (checking normalisation)

**p-adic BSD at $p = 3$ (supersingular):** Use plus/minus decomposition.
- $\hat{h}_3(P)$: computed via Besser's algorithm, $\hat{h}_3(P) \in 3\mathbb{Z}_3$ (valuation 1)
- $\text{Reg}_3(E) = \hat{h}_3(P)$ (rank 1)
- $L_3'(E, 1) = \mathcal{E}_3(E) \cdot |\Sha[3^\infty]| \cdot \text{Reg}_3 \cdot c_{37}^{(3)} / |E(\mathbb{Q})_{\text{tors}}|^2$

**p-adic BSD at $p = 7$ (ordinary):**
- $\hat{h}_7(P) \in \mathbb{Z}_7$ (a 7-adic unit or near-unit)
- $\text{Reg}_7(E) = \hat{h}_7(P)$
- $\mathcal{E}_7(E) = 1$ (no exceptional zero, since $a_7 \neq 1$)
- $L_7(E, 1) = \frac{|\Sha[7^\infty]| \cdot \text{Reg}_7 \cdot c_{37}^{(7)}}{|E(\mathbb{Q})_{\text{tors}}|^2}$

**Comparison:** $\text{Reg}_7 / \text{Reg}_\infty \approx \Omega_p(E) / \Omega_E^+ \cdot (\text{7-adic unit})$.

**Additional curve: $E = 11a1$** (rank 0, conductor 11).
- $L(E, 1) \approx 0.25384$
- At $p = 5$ (ordinary): $L_5(E, 1) = (1 - 1/\alpha_5)(1 - 5/\beta_5) \cdot L(E,1)/\Omega_E^+$
- $\alpha_5 = a_5 = -2$, so $(1 - 1/(-2)) = 3/2$
- $L_5(E, 1) = (3/2) \cdot (1 - 5/\beta_5) \cdot 0.2$
- p-adic BSD: $L_5(E, 1) = |\Sha[5^\infty]| \cdot c_{11}^{(5)} / |E(\mathbb{Q})_{\text{tors}}|^2 = 1/25$ (if $\Sha[5^\infty] = 1$, $c_{11} = 1$, tors = 5... actually $E(\mathbb{Q})_{\text{tors}} \cong \mathbb{Z}/5\mathbb{Z}$ for 11a1)
- So $L_5(E, 1) \stackrel{?}{=} 1/25$ (as a 5-adic number, $v_5(L_5(E,1)) = -2$?)

## Obstacle Analysis

1. **p-adic period:** The comparison $\text{Reg}_p / \text{Reg}_\infty = \Omega_p / \Omega_E \cdot (\text{unit})$ requires the p-adic period $\Omega_p(E)$, defined via $p$-adic Hodge theory (Fontaine's $B_{\text{dR}}$). Making this explicit and computable is the main obstruction.

2. **Tamagawa numbers:** The p-adic Tamagawa number $c_\ell^{(p)}$ is the p-part of the standard Tamagawa number. For primes of bad reduction, $c_\ell$ depends on the component group of the Néron model; extracting the p-part requires careful analysis.

3. **Sha computation:** $|\Sha[p^\infty]|$ is not directly computable (it requires verifying that certain Selmer classes are everywhere locally trivial). Descent methods give upper bounds; the p-BSD conjecture predicts the exact value.

4. **Rank $\geq 2$:** For $r \geq 2$, the p-adic regulator matrix $\det(\hat{h}_p(P_i, P_j))$ may have small p-adic valuation due to cancellations, making numerical verification difficult.

## Cross-Group Connections

- **Group A (Classical BSD):** p-BSD is the p-adic refinement; the two are formally equivalent under the comparison of periods, regulators, and Tamagawa numbers.
- **Group C (Iwasawa Theory):** The Iwasawa main conjecture is the algebraic backbone of p-BSD; Kato's Euler system and Skinner–Urban provide the main tools.
- **E-003 (MTT L-invariant):** When an exceptional zero occurs, the p-BSD formula requires the correction factor $\mathcal{L}_p(E)$.
- **E-007 (p-adic regulators):** The p-adic regulator $\text{Reg}_p(E)$ is the key analytic quantity in p-BSD.

## Classification
**Main conjecture / comparison.** The central formulation connecting p-adic L-values to arithmetic invariants.
