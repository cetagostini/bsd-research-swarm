# E-011 · p-adic Stark Conjectures and Their Relation to BSD

## Direction ID and Title
E-011: The p-adic Stark conjecture (Dasgupta–Kakde, Spies), Stark units, and their connection to p-adic BSD for CM elliptic curves.

## Status Assessment
**Resolved for totally real fields (Dasgupta–Kakde 2022); active for CM fields and non-CM elliptic curves.** Gross (1981) formulated the p-adic Stark conjecture for CM fields. Dasgupta–Kakde proved it for totally real fields. The connection to p-BSD for CM curves (via Rubin's main conjecture) is established in principle.

## Testable Conjecture or Lemma

**Theorem (Dasgupta–Kakde, 2022).** Let $F$ be a totally real field and $\mathfrak{p}$ a prime of $F$ above $p$. For each character $\chi$ of $\text{Gal}(F^{\text{ab}}/F)$, there exists a $p$-adic unit $\varepsilon_\chi \in \mathbb{C}_p^\times$ (a Stark unit) such that:
$$L_p'(\chi, 0) = \left(1 - \frac{\chi(\mathfrak{p})}{N\mathfrak{p}}\right) \log_p(\varepsilon_\chi)$$

**Conjecture (p-adic Stark for CM fields, Gross 1981).** Let $F$ be an imaginary quadratic field with discriminant $D_F$, $p$ a prime split in $F$ as $\mathfrak{p}\bar{\mathfrak{p}}$. For a ring class character $\chi$ of $F$:
$$L_p'(\chi, 0) = \left(1 - \frac{\chi(\mathfrak{p})}{p}\right) \log_p(\varepsilon_\chi)$$
where $\varepsilon_\chi$ is the $\chi$-component of a $p$-adic unit constructed from elliptic units.

**Conjecture (p-adic Stark → p-BSD for CM curves).** Let $E/\mathbb{Q}$ be an elliptic curve with CM by $\mathcal{O}_F$, $F$ imaginary quadratic, $p$ split in $F$. Then:
1. The Stark unit $\varepsilon_\chi$ generates the $p$-part of the Selmer group for the character $\chi$.
2. For rank 0: $L_p(E, 1) = (1 - a_p/p + 1/p) \cdot \log_p(\varepsilon_1)$ where $\varepsilon_1$ is the trivial-character Stark unit.
3. For rank 1: $L_p'(E, 1) = \hat{h}_p(P_{\varepsilon})$ where $P_{\varepsilon}$ is the point on $E$ corresponding to the Stark unit.

## Approach Summary

1. **Elliptic units:** For $F$ imaginary quadratic, the elliptic units $\theta_\chi \in F^{\text{ab},\times}$ are constructed from values of the Weber function at CM points of elliptic curves. These are the CM analogue of cyclotomic units.

2. **Katz p-adic L-function:** The Katz p-adic L-function $L_p^{\text{Katz}}(\chi, s)$ interpolates Hecke L-values $L(\chi, 0)$ at $s = 0$ for ring class characters $\chi$. The p-adic Stark conjecture asserts $L_p^{\text{Katz},\prime}(\chi, 0) = (\text{Euler factor}) \cdot \log_p(\varepsilon_\chi)$.

3. **Rubin's main conjecture:** For $F$ imaginary quadratic, Rubin proved the Iwasawa main conjecture: the characteristic ideal of the Selmer group of $E/F$ over the anticyclotomic $\mathbb{Z}_p$-extension is generated by $L_p^{\text{Katz}}$. This connects Stark units to Selmer groups.

4. **Darmon–Vonk (Stark–Heegner points):** For non-CM curves $E/\mathbb{Q}$ and a non-split prime $p$, the Stark–Heegner point $P_{\text{SH}} \in E(\mathbb{Q})$ is constructed via p-adic integration on $\text{GL}_2(\mathbb{Q}_p)$. The conjecture: $P_{\text{SH}}$ is a global point on $E$ satisfying a p-adic Gross–Zagier formula.

## Computational Example

**CM curve: $E = 27a1$** ($y^2 + y = x^3 - 7$, conductor 27, CM by $\mathbb{Z}[\omega]$, $\omega = (-1+\sqrt{-3})/2$).

$F = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$. Primes: $p = 7$ splits in $F$ as $\mathfrak{p}\bar{\mathfrak{p}}$ (since $7 \equiv 1 \pmod{3}$).

**Elliptic unit construction:**
- The CM elliptic curve with period lattice $\mathcal{O}_F = \mathbb{Z}[\omega]$ has $j$-invariant $j = 0$.
- The Weber function $h: E(\mathbb{C}) \to F^{\text{ab}}$ evaluated at torsion points gives elliptic units.
- $\varepsilon = h(P_{\mathfrak{p}})$ where $P_{\mathfrak{p}}$ is a $\mathfrak{p}$-torsion point.

**Katz p-adic L-function at $p = 7$:**
- $L_p^{\text{Katz}}(\chi, s)$ for $\chi = 1$ (trivial character): $L_p^{\text{Katz}}(1, 0) = (1 - 1/\mathfrak{p}) \cdot L(1, 0) = 0$ (since $L(1, 0) = 0$ for the trivial Hecke character of an imaginary quadratic field... actually $L(\chi_0, 0)$ for the trivial character of $F$ is related to $\zeta_F(0)$).
- The derivative: $L_p^{\text{Katz},\prime}(1, 0) = (1 - 1/p) \cdot \log_p(\varepsilon_1)$.

**p-adic Stark verification:**
- Compute $\varepsilon_1$ (the trivial-character elliptic unit) as a 7-adic number.
- $\log_7(\varepsilon_1)$ is a 7-adic number.
- Verify: $L_p^{\text{Katz},\prime}(1, 0) = (1 - 1/7) \cdot \log_7(\varepsilon_1)$ to precision $O(7^5)$.

**Connection to p-BSD:**
- $E = 27a1$ has rank 0. $L(E, 1) \neq 0$.
- p-BSD: $L_7(E, 1) = |\Sha[7^\infty]| \cdot c_{27}^{(7)} / |E(\mathbb{Q})_{\text{tors}}|^2$.
- The Stark unit $\varepsilon_1$ conjecturally satisfies $L_7(E, 1) = (1 - 1/7) \cdot \log_7(\varepsilon_1)$ (up to explicit factors).

**Rank 1 CM example: $E = 49a1$** ($y^2 + xy = x^3 - x^2 - 2x - 1$, conductor 49, CM by $\mathbb{Z}[\frac{1+\sqrt{-7}}{2}]$).
- $F = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-7})$. Rank 1.
- At $p = 2$: splits in $F$ ($2 = \mathfrak{p}\bar{\mathfrak{p}}$ since $-7 \equiv 1 \pmod{8}$, so $\left(\frac{-7}{2}\right) = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = 1$). Actually $-7 \equiv 1 \pmod{8}$ so 2 splits.
- The Stark unit $\varepsilon$ generates the Selmer group. The point $P_\varepsilon$ on $E$ (via the CM parametrisation) generates $E(\mathbb{Q}) \cong \mathbb{Z}$.
- p-adic Stark: $L_2'(E, 1) = \hat{h}_2(P_\varepsilon) \cdot (\text{correction})$.

## Obstacle Analysis

1. **Non-CM curves:** The p-adic Stark conjecture is formulated for CM fields. For non-CM elliptic curves $E/\mathbb{Q}$, the Stark–Heegner point construction (Darmon–Vonk) provides a p-adic analogue, but it relies on p-adic integration on $\text{GL}_2(\mathbb{Q}_p)$ that is not fully rigorous.

2. **Imaginary quadratic base field:** Dasgupta–Kakde proves the p-adic Stark conjecture for totally real $F$. Extending to imaginary quadratic $F$ (the setting for CM elliptic curves) requires different techniques — specifically, Rubin's main conjecture handles the algebraic side but the analytic construction of Stark units in the CM case is less direct.

3. **Heegner hypothesis:** The p-adic Stark → p-BSD connection requires $p$ to split in $F$ (so the p-adic L-function is well-defined). For inert primes, the construction requires modification (anticyclotomic p-adic L-functions).

4. **Darmon–Vonk construction:** The Stark–Heegner point $P_{\text{SH}}$ is defined via p-adic integration on $\text{GL}_2(\mathbb{Q}_p)$ using a specific choice of "integration cycle." Showing that $P_{\text{SH}}$ is a global point (not just a local p-adic point) requires a reciprocity law that is not yet proven.

## Cross-Group Connections

- **Group A (Classical BSD):** The p-adic Stark conjecture is the p-adic refinement of the classical Stark conjecture, which is related to the rank-0/rank-1 cases of BSD for CM curves.
- **Group B (Heegner Points):** For CM curves, the Stark unit corresponds to a Heegner point under the CM parametrisation. The p-adic Stark formula is a p-adic Gross–Zagier formula for CM curves.
- **E-002 (p-adic Gross–Zagier):** The p-adic Stark conjecture for rank 1 is a special case of the p-adic Gross–Zagier formula for CM curves.
- **E-005 (p-adic BSD):** The p-adic Stark conjecture provides the rank-0/rank-1 input for p-BSD in the CM case.
