# B-010: Euler Systems and Iwasawa Main Conjectures

## Direction ID and Title
B-010 — Euler Systems and Iwasawa Main Conjectures

## Status Assessment
The Iwasawa Main Conjecture (IMC) has been proved by Euler system methods in several key cases: Mazur-Wiles for $T = \mathbb{Z}_p(1)$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ (cyclotomic units), Rubin for CM elliptic curves (elliptic units), and conditionally by Kato/Skinner-Urban for weight-2 modular forms at $p = 3$. The $\subseteq$ direction (Euler system bound divides the $p$-adic L-function) is unconditional in all cases. The $\supseteq$ direction (converse) requires either $p$-adic Hodge-theoretic input or Galois deformation theory. The IMC for supersingular primes, totally real fields $F \neq \mathbb{Q}$, and $GL(n)$ with $n \geq 3$ remains open.

## Testable Conjecture or Lemma

**Conjecture B-010.** Let $T$ be a $p$-adic representation of $G_F$ (for $F$ a number field) and $F_\infty/F$ a $\mathbb{Z}_p$-extension with Iwasawa algebra $\Lambda = \mathbb{Z}_p[[\operatorname{Gal}(F_\infty/F)]]$. Assume $T$ admits an Euler system $\{c_m\}$ with associated $p$-adic L-function $L_p(T) \in \Lambda$. Then:

(a) **IMC divisibility (Euler system direction):**
$$(L_p(T)) \subseteq \operatorname{char}_\Lambda\left(\operatorname{Sel}(T/F_\infty)^\vee\right)$$

(b) **Full IMC:** Under the hypotheses:
   - $F = \mathbb{Q}$ or a totally real field with Leopoldt conjecture known,
   - $T = T_p(E)$ for a modular elliptic curve $E$ with good ordinary reduction at $p$,
   - $a_p \not\equiv p + 1 \pmod{p^2}$,
   - GRH for all $L$-functions of number fields,
$$\operatorname{char}_\Lambda\left(\operatorname{Sel}(T/F_\infty)^\vee\right) = (L_p(T))$$

(c) **BSD specialization:** If the IMC holds, then specializing at the augmentation ideal $\omega$:
$$\operatorname{ord}_p\left(\frac{L^{(r)}(E,1)}{r! \cdot \Omega_E}\right) = \operatorname{ord}_p\left(\frac{|\operatorname{III}[p^\infty]| \cdot \prod c_v^{(p)}}{|E(\mathbb{Q})_{\mathrm{tors}}|_p^2}\right)$$

(d) **Greenberg conjecture:** $\mu = 0$ for the Selmer group $\operatorname{Sel}(T/F_\infty)$ (the $\mu$-invariant of the characteristic ideal is zero).

## Approach Summary

The proof of the IMC proceeds in two distinct steps for the two inclusions. The $\subseteq$ direction is purely Euler-system-theoretic: the Bockstein map from the Euler system to the Selmer group produces the characteristic ideal divisibility. This is unconditional and works for any $T$ with an Euler system.

The $\supseteq$ direction requires constructing a "converse" Euler system or using an independent method to bound the Selmer group from below. For $T = \mathbb{Z}_p(1)$ (Mazur-Wiles), the lower bound comes from class field theory: the $p$-part of the class group of $\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{p^n})$ has $\Lambda$-rank equal to the order of vanishing of the Kubota-Leopoldt $p$-adic L-function. For CM curves (Rubin), the elliptic units provide both directions simultaneously because the CM theory gives a complete description of the Galois cohomology.

For non-CM curves, Skinner-Urban's approach uses the Eisenstein ideal: the Hecke algebra $\mathbb{T}$ acting on the space of modular forms of level $Np^n$ has an Eisenstein ideal $\mathfrak{E}$ generated by $T_\ell - 1 - \ell$ for $\ell \nmid Np$ and $U_p - 1$. The quotient $\mathbb{T}/\mathfrak{E}$ controls both the Selmer group and the $p$-adic L-function, yielding equality.

## Computational Example

- **$T = \mathbb{Z}_3(1)$, $F = \mathbb{Q}$**: The classical main conjecture. $L_3(s) = (1 - 1/3)(1 - 3^{s-1})\zeta(s)$ is the Kubota-Leopoldt function. Specializing at $s = 0$: $L_3(0) = (1 - 1/3)(-1/2) = -1/3$. This encodes $h(\mathbb{Q}(\mu_3)) = 1$ and $|\operatorname{Cl}(\mathbb{Q}(\mu_3))[3^\infty]| = 1$. ✓
- **$T = T_3(E)$, $E = $ 11a1**: $p = 3$. $a_3 = -2$. The IMC predicts $\operatorname{char}_\Lambda(\operatorname{Sel}^\vee) = (\mathcal{L}_3^{\mathrm{Kato}})$. Compute both sides: $\mathcal{L}_3^{\mathrm{Kato}}$ as a power series in $\mathbb{Z}_3[[T]]$, and $\operatorname{Sel}(E/\mathbb{Q}_\infty)$ via Magma's Iwasawa theory package.
- **$T = T_5(E)$, $E = $ 37a1**: Test the IMC specialization at $s = 1$: the leading coefficient of $\mathcal{L}_5$ should match $L'(E,1)/\Omega \cdot |\operatorname{III}| \cdot \prod c_v / |E_{\mathrm{tors}}|^2$.

## Obstacle Analysis

The $\supseteq$ direction for general $p$ and non-CM curves is the fundamental unsolved problem. The Eisenstein ideal technique of Skinner-Urban is specific to $p = 3$ because the congruence $\binom{p}{1} \equiv 0 \pmod{p}$ (used to construct the Eisenstein ideal) only produces the right structure at small primes. For $p \geq 5$, the congruence ideal $\mathcal{C}$ has $p$-adic valuation that makes the argument imprecise. The supersingular case requires the Kobayashi $\pm$-theory, where the Iwasawa algebra has a different (non-commutative) structure, and the main conjecture formulation is more subtle.
