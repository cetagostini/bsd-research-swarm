# Group F: Motivic and Cohomological Methods

## Mission

Apply motivic cohomology, algebraic K-theory, syntomic cohomology, Beilinson's framework of motivic complexes and regulator maps, and the Bloch–Kato conjecture program to the Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture. This group studies the deep cohomological structures underlying L-functions, special values, regulators, and Selmer/Shafarevich–Tate groups through the lens of motivic and arithmetic cohomology theories.

## Scope

Group F operates at the intersection of algebraic K-theory, motivic cohomology, syntomic and Deligne cohomology, arithmetic intersection theory, and the structural cohomological formalisms (Deninger's program) that seek to place L-functions in a unified geometric framework. The directions range from concrete computational questions (regulator maps from K_2, Soulé regulators) to broad structural programs (Bloch–Kato conjecture, motivic L-functions, Deninger's cohomological formalism).
