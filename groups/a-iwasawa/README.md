# Group A: Iwasawa Theory

## Mission

Explore p-adic approaches to BSD via Iwasawa theory, p-adic L-functions, and Selmer groups. Develop the Iwasawa-theoretic machinery necessary to understand the p-adic variation of Selmer groups in towers of number fields and relate their structure to the p-adic interpolation of L-values.
