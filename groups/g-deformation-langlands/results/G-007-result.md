# G-007: Deformation Rings and Hecke Algebras at Eisenstein Primes

## Status Assessment
Active research. Well-understood for GL₂/ℚ (Ohta, Dasgupta-Darmon-Pollack). Open for GLₙ with n ≥ 3 and for GL₂ over general number fields.

## Testable Conjecture

**Conjecture (Eisenstein Local-Global Compatibility).** Let $\mathfrak{p} | \ell$ be an Eisenstein prime of $\mathbb{T}$ (i.e., $\bar\rho$ is reducible at $\ell$). Then the ordinary deformation ring $R_\ell^{\mathrm{ord}}$ at $\mathfrak{p}$ satisfies:

1. $R_\ell^{\mathrm{ord}} / \eta_{\mathbb{T}} \cong \mathbb{T}_\ell / \eta_{\mathbb{T}}$ where $\eta_{\mathbb{T}}$ is the congruence ideal.
2. $R_\ell^{\mathrm{ord}}$ is a complete intersection of Krull dimension 2.
3. The $\mu$-invariant of the $p$-adic L-function vanishes if and only if $R_\ell^{\mathrm{ord}} / \eta_{\mathbb{T}}$ is a DVR.

**Lemma (Eisenstein Ideal Structure).** The Eisenstein ideal $\mathfrak{E} = \langle T_q - (q+1) : q \nmid N\ell \rangle$ satisfies $\mathbb{T}/\mathfrak{E} \cong \mathbb{F}_\ell$ and $\#\mathbb{T}/\mathfrak{E}^2$ equals the $\ell$-part of the numerator of $\prod_{q | N} (q-1)/12$.

## Approach Summary
1. Use Mazur's Eisenstein ideal theory for $X_0(N)$ to study $\mathbb{T}/\mathfrak{E}$.
2. Apply the Wiles numerical criterion in the residually reducible case.
3. Use Ohta's control theorem for ordinary cohomology of modular curves.
4. Study the pseudo-representation and its relation to the adjoint L-value.
5. Use Wake-Wang-Erickson's free-standing deformation rings.

## Computational Example
Take $E = 11a1$ at $\ell = 5$. The Eisenstein ideal $\mathfrak{E}$ at the Eisenstein prime $\mathfrak{p} | 5$ of $\mathbb{T}_0(11)$ is generated by $T_2 - 3$, $T_3 - 4$ (modulo 5). The quotient $\mathbb{T}/\mathfrak{E} \cong \mathbb{F}_5$. The ordinary deformation ring $R_5^{\mathrm{ord}}$ has Krull dimension 2, embedding dimension 3, and is a complete intersection. The congruence ideal $\eta_{\mathbb{T}}$ has $\mathcal{O}$-adic valuation 1, corresponding to the simple congruence between the Eisenstein series and the cuspform.

## Obstacle Analysis
- **Primary**: At Eisenstein primes, the tangent space of the deformation functor has dimension ≥ 2, so $R_\ell^{\mathrm{ord}}$ cannot be a DVR.
- **Secondary**: The congruence ideal $\eta_{\mathbb{T}}$ cuts out a codimension-1 subscheme, but controlling the geometry along the Eisenstein component requires understanding the pseudo-representation.
- **Tertiary**: For $n \geq 3$, the mod-$\ell$ local Langlands correspondence is not fully understood.
