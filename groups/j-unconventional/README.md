# Group J: Unconventional and Emerging Approaches

## Mission

Explore non-traditional and emerging mathematical frameworks for approaching the Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture. This group investigates connections to mirror symmetry, perfectoid spaces, categorification, the Kudla program, machine learning, and other frontier areas that may provide novel pathways to understanding L-functions and ranks of elliptic curves.
